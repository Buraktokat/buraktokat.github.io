Official Artist Biography

BURAK TOKAT.

Burak Tokat is a Turkish DJ and electronic music producer based in Antalya, Türkiye. Active since 2020, his work is shaped by melodic atmosphere, hypnotic rhythm and modern club energy, moving between Melodic Techno, Melodic House and Afro House.

Artist Profile

Tokat began his DJ career in 2020. His selected club experience includes Club Inferno Mania in Konya and Bursa and Mr. Frog in Konya. Alongside DJ performance, he develops original electronic music with an emphasis on emotional melodic writing and club-focused arrangement.

His released catalogue includes Feels Like via Surrealismo Records. His upcoming Melodic Techno single Crescent is scheduled for release on Colorica on 6 November 2026, with pre-order beginning 23 October 2026.
