Official Artist Biography

BURAK TOKAT.

Burak Tokat is a Turkish DJ and electronic music producer based in Antalya, Türkiye. Active since 2020, his work brings together club performance, professional hospitality entertainment experience and original electronic music production.

Artist Profile

Background

Tokat began his DJ career in 2020. His live background includes Club Inferno Mania in Konya and Bursa as well as selected experience at Mr. Frog in Konya.

His professional hospitality background includes experience within music and entertainment operations at Rixos Premium Belek in Antalya, working around varied event concepts, guest profiles and operational teams.

His current production focus is Melodic Techno and Melodic House. Afro House remains part of selected DJ sets and remix projects.
