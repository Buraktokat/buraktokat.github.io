02 — Official Biography

Biography.

Burak Tokat is a Turkish DJ and electronic music producer based in Antalya, Türkiye. Active since 2020, he developed his background through the Turkish club scene before placing a stronger focus on original music production.

His sound moves primarily between Melodic Techno and Melodic House, combining atmospheric melodies, hypnotic grooves and modern club energy. Tokat is currently entering a new stage of his artist project, with a stronger international focus and an expanding catalog of original productions.

His upcoming single “Crescent” is scheduled for release through Colorica on 6 November 2026, following a pre-order launch on 23 October 2026.
