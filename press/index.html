Artist Bio

Burak Tokat is a Turkish DJ and electronic music producer based in Antalya. Active since 2020, his background includes club performance, hospitality entertainment operations and original electronic music production.

His current production direction focuses on Melodic Techno and Melodic House, while Afro House remains part of selected DJ sets and remix projects.
