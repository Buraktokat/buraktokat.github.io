02 — Official Biography

Biography.

Burak Tokat is a Turkish DJ and electronic music producer based in Antalya, Türkiye. Active since 2020, he developed his live background through club environments including Club Inferno Mania across Konya and Bursa and Mr Frog in Konya.

His professional experience also includes work within the music and entertainment operations of Rixos Premium Belek, including adapting musical programming to diverse international guest profiles and event concepts while maintaining professional stage standards and coordinating with operational and entertainment teams.

Tokat's current production direction focuses primarily on Melodic Techno and Melodic House, with selected Afro House and tribal influences remaining part of his wider DJ and production identity.
