03 / Bio

THE
ARTIST

Burak Tokat is a DJ and electronic music producer from Türkiye.

His musical direction moves between Melodic Techno, Melodic House and Afro House, combining emotional melodies with deep grooves, atmospheric textures and late-night electronic energy.

As both a DJ and producer, Burak Tokat focuses on creating immersive electronic music experiences built around rhythm, atmosphere and movement.
