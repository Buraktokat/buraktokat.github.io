02 / Bio

BURAK

Burak Tokat is a DJ and electronic music producer from Türkiye.

His musical direction moves between Melodic Techno, Melodic House and Afro House, combining atmospheric melodies, deep grooves and modern electronic energy.

His work as a DJ and producer focuses on creating immersive electronic music experiences built around rhythm, atmosphere and movement.
