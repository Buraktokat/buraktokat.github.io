02 — Artist

THE ARTIST.

Burak Tokat is a Turkish DJ and electronic music producer based in Antalya. Active since 2020, his background includes club performance and music/entertainment operations experience in hospitality environments.

His current production direction focuses primarily on Melodic Techno and Melodic House, with Afro House appearing in selected DJ sets and remix projects.
